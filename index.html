📄 Terms & Conditions

⏰ Submission Window
Weekly Challenge submissions open every Friday at 8:00 PM (IST) and remain valid until Monday at 8:00 AM (IST). Any submission made after this time will not be considered, even if it is accepted due to a technical issue.

⚠️ Late / Technical Submissions
If a submission is recorded after Monday 8:00 AM (IST) due to any technical reason, it will still be treated as invalid. EquitySkill’s system time will be the final authority.

📧 Submission Confirmation
A confirmation email will be sent after every valid submission. This email is mandatory proof for prize claims. No prize will be processed without the submission confirmation email.

🔐 One Entry Rule
Only one submission per phone number and email ID is allowed for each weekly challenge. If multiple entries are detected, all related submissions may be disqualified.

📊 Scoring & Results
Points calculation is fully rule-based and automated. EquitySkill’s scoring and results will be final and binding.

🏆 Prize Distribution
Prizes are distributed based on score categories:

55 points → ₹15,000
50 points → ₹10,000
45 points → ₹5,000

If multiple winners fall under the same score category, the prize amount will be equally divided among all winners.

Example:
55 points → 2 winners → ₹7,500 each
45 points → 4 winners → ₹1,250 each

⚖️ Fair Play Policy
Any form of manipulation, automation, or unfair practice may result in permanent disqualification. EquitySkill reserves the right to cancel any entry without prior notice.

🚫 No Investment Advice
This contest is a skill-based game. EquitySkill does not provide any investment advice or stock recommendations.

🏛 Final Authority
All decisions made by EquitySkill will be final. Terms & Conditions may be updated in the future without prior notice.
